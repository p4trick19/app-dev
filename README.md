# Captain America: The First Avenger

**Captain America** is one of the most iconic superheroes from the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU).

## Main Points

- Created by Joe Simon and Jack Kirby in 1941.
- Portrayed by *Chris Evans* in the MCU.
- Real name: Steve Rogers.

> "I'm just a kid from Brooklyn." – Steve Rogers (Captain America)

### Reasons Why I Love Captain America

1. Inspiring leadership qualities
2. Strong sense of justice and morality
3. Incredible action sequences

---

### Key Allies

- Iron Man
- Black Widow
- The Winter Soldier

You can learn more about Captain America [here](https://www.marvel.com/characters/captain-america-steve-rogers/on-screen).
